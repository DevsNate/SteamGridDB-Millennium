SteamGridDB's artwork browser, running natively in Steam on Windows through [Millennium](https://steambrew.app). Based on [decky-steamgriddb](https://github.com/SteamGridDB/decky-steamgriddb) 1.8.1.

## Highlights

- Browse and apply SteamGridDB artwork from Big Picture, or in a matching window on the Steam desktop, through "Change Artwork..." in a game's context menu.
- Icons for Steam games and non-Steam shortcuts, local files, the logo positioner, square capsules, matching recents capsule and capsule glow.
- Add artwork to your SteamGridDB collections, or open it on SteamGridDB, from its details.
- Hide Authors, on by default.
- A size slider on every tab, and Reset puts back a Steam game's original icon.

## Install

1. Download `steamgriddb.star` below.
2. Put it in `Steam\millennium\plugins\`.
3. Restart Steam and enable SteamGridDB in Millennium's plugin settings.

## Requirements

- A Starlight-capable Millennium (verified with v3.6.2).
- Your own free [SteamGridDB API Key](https://www.steamgriddb.com/profile/preferences/api).
- In Big Picture, the settings page is reached through the [Plugins Hub](https://github.com/DevsNate/PluginsHub-Millennium) plugin. On the desktop, it is in Millennium's plugin settings.
